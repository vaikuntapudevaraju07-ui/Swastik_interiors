Transparency & Governance

Frequently Addressed Inquiries

We adhere to a zero-hidden-cost guarantee. Following our schematic design phase, you receive an itemized bill of quantities with granular line items across civil structural works, bespoke German joinery, electrical automations, and designer upholstery. All factory-direct procurement discounts are passed transparently onto your quotation.

Our precision modular millwork is manufactured off-site in our state-of-the-art facility while on-site civil works run concurrently. Once civil finishes are signed off, our turnkey fit-out locks into an insured 45-day timeline. Any unforeseen delay triggers a documented compensatory penalty paid directly to the homeowner.

Every project includes an embossed Certificate of Warranty guaranteeing all structural carcass cabinetry, anti-bubble laminate adhesion, Austrian Blum soft-close mechanics, and termite-resistant marine plywood for up to 10 full years, alongside complimentary bi-annual maintenance audits.
